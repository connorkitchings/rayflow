# Phish — 2015 Summer Tour / Magnaball Rig

**Band:** Phish
**Year:** 2015 (Summer Tour, culminating in Magnaball festival)
**LD:** Chris Kuroda
**Programmer:** Andrew Giffin
**Console:** grandMA2 full-size

## Era Context

2015 was a significant year for Phish's production. Following their 2009 reunion, the rig had grown substantially. By 2015, Kuroda had integrated video elements alongside the massive moving light rig, and the Magnaball festival (Watkins Glen, August 2015) featured an expanded festival-only rig configuration.

## Truss Structure

```
Configuration: 5 horizontal trusses + side booms + floor package

  FOH Truss:  Over audience, ~50-60 ft from stage. Spots and profiles for key light.
  DS Truss:   Downstage, directly over stage front edge.
  Mid Truss:  Mid-stage. Primary beam and wash positions.
  US Truss:   Upstage. Backlight and aerial beam positions.
  Rear Truss: Further upstage. Video wall support and rear lighting.

  SL Booms: Stage Left vertical pipes (multiple heights)
  SR Booms: Stage Right vertical pipes (multiple heights)
  Floor: LED washes, blinders, haze
```

## Distinguishing Features

- **Kuroda's signature color vocabulary:** Bold, saturated colors — deep blues, rich ambers, vivid magentas. Stark warm/cool contrasts.
- **Massive fixture count:** The Phish rig is famously large. This gives Kuroda every color, angle, and beam type available instantly without waiting for fixture changes.
- **Independent zone control:** Kuroda colors different stage zones independently — Stage Left might be warm while Stage Right is cool. This requires enough fixtures per zone for independent control.
- **Speed master mastery:** All chases and movements linked to a speed master tapped to the band's tempo. When Phish accelerates, the lights accelerate.
- **The Kuroda Blackout:** Sudden, complete blackout held for moments, then full-rig explosion on the downbeat. His signature dynamic move.
- **Video integration (2015):** The 2015 era introduced more video elements — an LED wall on the rear truss and possibly side screens — that Kuroda integrated with the lighting rather than treating as a separate department.
- **Magnaball Special Rig:** The festival rig was larger than the standard tour rig, with additional fixtures, expanded truss, and festival-only positions.
