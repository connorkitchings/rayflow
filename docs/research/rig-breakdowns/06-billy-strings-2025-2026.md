## The Cross-Genre Challenge

Billy Strings presents a unique lighting challenge: the music is rooted in acoustic bluegrass (warm, natural, intimate) but regularly launches into psychedelic, effects-heavy improvisational jams. Waller must serve both:
- **Bluegrass moments:** Warm amber washes, minimal movement, natural palettes, clear front light on the band's acoustic instruments
- **Psychedelic jams:** Saturated colors, aggressive movement, aerial beams, effects-driven visual chaos

The rig must support both modes and transition convincingly between them — often within the same song.

## Distinguishing Features

- **Dual-mode design:** The rig must excel at both warm acoustic lighting and aggressive psychedelic effects. This means sufficient front light for the bluegrass material AND enough beams and effects for the jams. The LD's skill is in transitioning between these modes convincingly.
- **Waller's cross-genre fluency:** Having lit both electronic pioneers (STS9) and acoustic phenom (Billy Strings), Waller is uniquely equipped for this dual-mode challenge. His busking layouts are built for rapid genre-switching — warm acoustic palette on one fader set, psychedelic effects on another.
- **Acoustic instrument lighting:** Bluegrass instruments (acoustic guitar, mandolin, banjo, fiddle, upright bass) need to be seen clearly. Front light must be precise, consistent, and flattering on wood instruments. Warm color temperature (3200K-4000K) is essential for natural instrument rendering.
- **The "Billy Peak":** Billy Strings shows build from intricate acoustic passages into full-band psychedelic peaks. Waller tracks this arc — sparse, warm lighting for the verses and composed sections, then expanding into saturation and movement as the jam intensifies.
- **Potential video integration:** By 2025–2026, arena-scale Billy Strings shows may include an LED video wall or scenic elements. Waller would integrate lighting with these elements, not compete with them.
- **Rental rig adaptability:** Billy Strings tours extensively. Waller's rental rig expertise (honed across STS9 and other acts) means the show file is designed for portability — universal palettes, fixture-group-agnostic cues, fast console setup at each venue.
