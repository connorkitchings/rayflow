## Hoffman Rig Philosophy (Synthesized from Quotes)

- **Broad dynamic range required:** "Hard rock to emotive ballads and stops at every station in between"
- **Theatrical approach for emotional material:** More nuanced than typical concert lighting
- **"Best of breed" fixture selection:** Hoffman picks best-in-category fixtures rather than brand loyalty. The 2019 rig included fixtures from Robe, GLP, Martin, and Vari-Lite simultaneously — four different manufacturers.
- **Flexibility and versatility as primary values:** Venue-tailored rigs (2019 one-offs), IP-rated for outdoor reliability (2021 Dominos)
- **Long-term vendor relationships:** Over a decade with Bandit Lites and Dizzy Gosnell. Built his own company (Pulse Lighting) for owned inventory.

---

## Distinguishing Features

- **Multi-manufacturer rig:** Unlike the early Waful (all-Martin) or some single-brand tours, Hoffman's rig mixes Robe, GLP, Martin, Vari-Lite, and Ayrton — selecting the best fixture for each role regardless of brand.
- **Venue-flexible design:** The 2019 one-off format allowed per-venue rig customization. Most tours standardize; Hoffman personalized.
- **IP65 awareness:** The Domino purchase was driven by outdoor reliability. Hoffman is pragmatically focused on gear that survives real touring conditions.
- **Theatrical sensibility:** "A more theatrical approach to the emotionally heavier music" — Hoffman brings a theatrical touch to concert lighting that reflects Panic's emotional range.
- **The LD as business owner:** Hoffman co-owns Pulse Lighting with ~150 Ayrton fixtures plus grandMA consoles. He's both the creative LD and the gear supplier — unusual in the industry.

## Notes

- Hoffman previously served as Panic's crew chief for 3 years before becoming LD in 2009. He knows the band's music and personnel deeply.
- Unlike newer LDs in the scene (Goedde, Caporale, Waller), Hoffman has been with one band for 15+ years. His rig reflects deep, long-term familiarity with one act's music.
