## Era Context

Umphrey's McGee's Iceland shows (March 2022, Reykjavik) were destination events — fans traveled to Iceland for a multi-night run at the Harpa concert hall. These were not standard tour stops; the rig likely differed from UM's US touring rig due to international shipping constraints.

## Truss Structure (Likely)

The Harpa venue is a modern concert hall with limited fly capacity compared to US sheds and arenas. The rig was likely:

```

  DS/Mid Truss: Over stage front
```

## Distinguishing Features

- **Condensed touring rig:** Destination shows meant a reduced fixture count. The Iceland shows likely used ~60% of the standard US touring rig.
- **Waful's hybrid approach:** Composed song sections were timecoded; improvised jams released to busking faders. The Iceland shows would have followed this same pattern.
- **Pixel mapping integration:** Waful was an early adopter of pixel-mapped LED fixtures in the jam scene. Even the condensed rig likely included pixel bars.
- **Intricate movement patterns:** Waful's style favors complex, interwoven movement across fixture groups rather than bold single-pattern motion.
- **Venue adaptation:** Harpa's architecture (a modern glass concert hall) is visually striking. The lighting likely integrated with the room's ambient character rather than overpowering it.
