# Mike Jaws — Eggy & Dopapod

**Era:** 2010s – present
**Role:** Lighting Director, Eggy (formerly Dopapod)

## The Synergistic Jam Aesthetic

Mike Jaws represents the next wave of improvisational lighting directors who bridge complex progressive-rock cues with open-ended, groove-oriented jams. Having developed his signature style lighting the progressive-fusion band **Dopapod**, Jaws transitioned to lighting the rising Connecticut-based jam quartet **Eggy**. 

His work is characterized by highly tasteful, curated color palettes, intricate volumetric beam structures, and a deep, intuitive alignment with the band's musical dynamics.

---

## Programming Style

### Precision and Accentuation
Jaws is renowned for his ability to hit both the macro dynamics (climax builds) and the micro accents (snare hits, keyboard trills) without cluttering the visual space:
* **Musical Peak Tracking:** Accentuates Eggy's storytelling jams by matching the visual intensity to the narrative arc of the song.
* **Beam-Forward Geometry:** Uses precise fan layouts and gobos to construct geometric shapes in the air, creating a volumetric "room" on stage that shifts with the musical key.
* **Contrasting Palettes:** Frequently employs dual-color opposition (e.g., warm pastel ambers combined with deep lavender or slate blue) that highlights individual musicians during solos.

### The Progressive Fusion Influence
His background with Dopapod—whose music features odd time signatures, rapid syncopated breaks, and heavy synthesizer textures—infused Jaws' programming with high-speed response capabilities. When lighting Eggy, he translates this technical precision into a more organic, fluid flow, allowing the lights to breathe during melodic improvisation.

---

## Technical Workflow

Like other modern designers, Jaws utilizes a hybrid programming workflow that relies heavily on pre-tour virtual preparation:

### Console and Layout
* **Console:** grandMA platform (using clean executor configurations with multiple cue-list layers).
* **Busking Architecture:** Uses a comprehensive speed master layout to sync chase rates to the drummer's tempo in real time, combined with override executors for strobe and color bumps.

## RayFlow Relevance

1. **Contrasting Color Scheming:** RayFlow should generate palettes that pair warm pastels with cool, saturated accents rather than relying solely on single-hue washes.
2. **Syncopated Bumps:** The cue-generation engine should support rapid, single-channel "bumps" that align with drum/keyboard transient peaks (snare hits or synth stabs).
3. **Improvisational Layering:** Rigs generated by RayFlow must feature clean separation between background wash fixtures (for color mood) and volumetric spot/beam fixtures (for geometric accentuation).
