## Professional Certification and Manufacturer Academies

- **The New School / Parsons School of Design:** Non-degree Lighting Design Certificate ($1,890). Three sequential courses: Lighting Fundamentals and Technologies, Lighting Design Criteria and Considerations, Designing a Lighting System. IDCEC-registered; 3-credit pathway to Parsons MFA Lighting Program.
- **The Illuminating Engineering Society (IES):** Fundamentals of Lighting course (FOL-23), energy-efficient design, LED-era maintenance, visual requirements for low-vision/senior populations.
- **NEMA Academy (LCA Education Express):** 67+ hours across 31 modules ($5 each or $160 full series). Topics: occupancy sensors, LED dimming, networked wireless controls, commercial energy code commissioning.
- **Lutron Lighting Control Institute (LCI):** AIA and USGBC-accredited CEUs. Focus: automated shade control, LED fixture performance, human-centric lighting, WELL v2 sustainability standards.
- **US DOD ESTCP Advanced Lighting Controls Program:** Free, public-domain. 16 sessions for facility managers, 17 technical courses for designers. Daylight-responsive tuning, task tuning, network security.
- **Acuity Academy:** Industry-specific webinars. Emphasis: healthcare environments, biophilic integration, operational energy savings.

## Practical Internships and Industry Volunteer Pipelines

- **IATSE Training Trust Fund (TTF):** OSHA-10 safety courses, AVIXA CTS exam prep, ETCP reimbursements for high-voltage and rigging certifications.
- **Regional Theatres (e.g., Aurora Theatre Academy, Alliance Theatre):** Technical theater programs and internships. Hands-on training on theatrical instruments, "tech bagels" sessions, smart moving fixture operation, live console operation.
- **Community Theatre League (CTL):** Structured volunteer environments. Progression from basic execution ("pushing a GO button") to designing entire shows with artistic directors.
