# Visual Textures, Movement, and Dynamic Punctuations

**Source:** `docs/research/manual_research2.txt`

## Core Concepts

Designers build visual depth by layering texture and movement over basic color washes. A **wash** provides smooth, even color across the stage to set overall mood but lacks texture.

### GOBOs (Goes Before Optics)

Thin stencils of metal or glass placed inside a profile fixture's optical path to project intricate patterns. Patterns range from natural breakups (leaves, branches) to geometric shapes and corporate branding.

### Beams and Scanners

High-intensity, narrow-beam fixtures (such as dedicated beam moving heads) project sharp shafts of light through the air.

### Atmospheric Haze

Microscopic particulate suspended in the air that makes projected beams visible. Without haze, beam effects remain invisible until they strike a physical surface.

### Ballyhoo (Bally)

A high-energy motion cue where automated fixtures sweep across the performance space and audience in fast, sweeping, figure-8 patterns. Accompanied by haze and rapid color or intensity shifts, ballyhoos are used during high-energy moments (walk-ons, musical climaxes) to build excitement. They are avoided during quiet, information-heavy moments where they would distract the audience.

### Sweeps, Fly-Outs, and Splays

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| Sweeps | Slide light beams uniformly across the stage |
| Fly-Outs | Tilt fixtures rapidly upward toward the sky while fading intensity to black |
| Splays ("God fingers") | Fan out fixtures radially to create stable, architectural shapes of light |

### Strobe Lights

High-intensity, rapid flashes producing a stop-motion visual effect, freezing performers' movements at regular intervals. Modern LED strobes provide precise control over flash rate, duration, color, and intensity. Must be managed carefully to protect audience members with photosensitive epilepsy.

### Audience Blinders

Floodlights aimed directly at the audience. Flashed momentarily on musical drops or final beats, blinders physically connect performers and crowd by illuminating the entire room.
